Doses & injections

Doses without memory games.

Set your day, log in two taps, and let streaks do the motivating. Your dose history and symptom log stay accurate when your provider asks about week three.

Every dose, logged and scheduled

  • Two-tap logging on your dose day — medication, dose, and injection site in one entry
  • A dose counter that shows months of history at a glance
  • Upcoming doses laid out ahead, with reminders on the day
  • Symptom check-ins tied to each dose, so patterns are easy to bring to appointments

Rotation, tracked on a body map

Every injection site is logged on a body map, with a recommendation for where to go next — so rotation happens by habit, not by memory.

  • Eight tracked injection sites across the body map
  • A recommended next site, based on where you've been
  • Site history alongside each logged dose

See each dose build and fade

The estimated levels chart draws how each dose accumulates and tapers over time — an estimate from your log, not a measurement, and clearly labeled as such in the app.

Works alongside lab tracking and weight trends, so your whole therapy record lives in one place.
