Medications

Built for the medication you're on.

Journey Tracker is purpose-built for GLP-1 therapy — Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, Zepbound®, compounded GLP-1s, and other semaglutide and tirzepatide medications. Pick yours during setup and the app follows your prescribed schedule.

Zepbound® & Mounjaro® (tirzepatide)

Log each tirzepatide dose with its strength, track your prescribed titration steps as your provider adjusts them, and keep injection sites rotating on the body map.

Wegovy® & Ozempic® (semaglutide)

Track your semaglutide doses on your prescribed schedule, with reminders on dose day and a symptom log that keeps week-by-week changes easy to recall at appointments.

Compounded GLP-1s

Flexible medication setup covers compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide — name, strength, and schedule the way your pharmacy dispenses it.

Other GLP-1 medications

The medication catalogue updates over the air, and custom entries cover anything else your provider prescribes — the tracking works the same way.

The same complete record, whichever you're on
