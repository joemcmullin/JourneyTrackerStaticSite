Labs

Labs, finally organized.

Every blood panel in one timeline — HbA1c, cholesterol, metabolic markers — side by side across your whole journey. Walk into appointments with the full picture.

Every lab test, its own trend line

  • Each marker — HbA1c, cholesterol, glucose, liver panels, and more — charted over time
  • Results shown against the reference range your provider reported, with the source attached
  • Follow the marker that matters most to you — it lives right on your Home screen

Three ways to get results in

  • Apple Health Records — connect your provider once and lab results import automatically
  • Scan a report — photograph or import a PDF; on-device text recognition reads the values
  • Type it in — quick manual entry for anything else

However they arrive, results land in one timeline you can scrub through, visit by visit.

Reports your provider will actually read

Turn your record into a clean PDF before each visit — labs, weight, and doses together — generated on your device and shared only when you choose to.
