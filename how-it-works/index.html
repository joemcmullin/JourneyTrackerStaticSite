Getting started

Up and running in minutes.

No account to create. No setup marathon. Just download and go.

1 · Download the app

Get Journey Tracker on the App Store for iPhone and iPad — one download covers both, with iCloud keeping them in step.

2 · Start your free trial

Every feature is unlocked for 7 days. No charge today, and Apple reminds you before the trial ends. Prefer not to? Continue with the free version.

3 · Make it yours

Set your goal weight, add your medication if you take one, and connect Apple Health — all optional.

4 · Track your journey

Log doses, weight, labs, and walks. Walk into every appointment with your story organized.

Track every signal

Weight, body composition, doses, symptoms, labs, photos, walks — two-tap logging and HealthKit sync mean the record keeps itself.

  • Home shows today at a glance: next dose, weight, walks, and your followed lab marker
  • Apple Health sync fills in weight and steps automatically
  • Spreadsheet import brings in history from Renpho, Withings, Garmin, and more

See what's working

Trends and weekly recaps turn months of entries into answers you can bring to your next appointment — and the Logbook keeps the whole journey in one scroll, days to years.

Celebrate every win

Milestones, streaks, mapped walks, and non-scale victories — because the jeans that fit again deserve a place in the record too.
